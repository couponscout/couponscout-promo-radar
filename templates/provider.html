How this page was produced

Nothing on this page was written or adjusted by hand. A scheduled job reads $name's own public pages - its product feed where one exists, and the schema.org offer data on its own product pages otherwise - and rebuilds this page every 6 hours. Where the brand publishes a reference price we can show a real percentage. Where it only files an item under its own sale page, we show the real price and label it as exactly that, with no percentage attached. If the brand stops publishing this data, this page shrinks rather than fills up.
