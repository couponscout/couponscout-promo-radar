About $site_title

$site_title is an independent price tracker for consumer brand promotions. It watches the official sale pages and public product feeds of $brand_count brands and republishes what those brands themselves published - the price, the reference price where one exists, and the exact page the number was read from. It does not write prices by hand and it does not estimate them.

Why the site exists

Brand sale pages are spread across ten or more separate shops, each with its own layout, its own idea of what "on sale" means and its own way of hiding the real price. Comparing them by hand means opening a dozen tabs and still not knowing whether the number you are looking at is a genuine discount or just the normal price wearing a badge. This site does that comparison mechanically, on a schedule, and shows its work: every listing names the page it was read from and the moment it was read.

How the data is produced

A scheduled job reads each brand's own public pages - the product feed where the brand publishes one, and the schema.org offer data on its own product pages otherwise. It rebuilds the whole site every 6 hours. There is no editorial step: nothing on a listing page is written or adjusted by hand. Two kinds of listing exist, and they are labelled differently on purpose:

  • Verified discountThe brand published a reference price, so the percentage is arithmetic on two published numbers.
  • On brand sale pageThe item sits on a page the brand itself calls a sale; we show the real price and claim no percentage, because there was no reference price to compute one from.

When a brand publishes no machine-readable offer data at all, it gets a page that links to its official promo page and nothing else. That is a deliberate choice: a page that says less is better than a page that invents more.
